About Sai CRT

Sai Campus Recruitment Training (Sai CRT) prepares students for top IT service and product-based companies, helping them become industry-ready professionals. We offer focused training in coding, Data Structures & Algorithms, Python, Data Science, Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence — aligned with what companies actually hire for.

We teach the complete placement stack: the aptitude, logical reasoning and English communication that written tests and interviews rely on, alongside strong technical foundations. Learn the way that suits you — in our Bhopal classroom or fully online, with class recordings so you never miss a session.

With 10,000+ students trained and 2000+ placed at top MNCs — backed by a 4.8★ rating from students — Sai CRT is a trusted name in career-oriented IT training in Bhopal.
